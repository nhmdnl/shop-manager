# Arch-SME features

Arch-SME is free and works fully offline, with no account. Optional paid cloud plans add the things that need a server: backups off the tablet, and seeing the shop when you're away.

The rule: **anything a shop needs to run an honest day stays free.**

## Free: in the app

No account, no internet needed.

**Selling**
- Product grid with categories and search, and a cart.
- Payment in cash, Mobile Money, or credit to a named customer.
- Quick cash amounts and change.
- A 6-second Undo after each sale.
- Today's sales, sale details, and voids with a reason.

**Stock and suppliers**
- Stock list with low-stock warnings.
- Purchases, paid now or not paid yet, from cash or Mobile Money.
- New and edited products, starting stock, and stock corrections.
- Barcode scanning with the camera in Sell, Stock, Edit product and purchases. Works offline.
- Suppliers, product links, a suggested reorder quantity, and a WhatsApp order message.

**The day's money**
- Open the day with a note counter. Selling, purchases and expenses stay locked until the day is open.
- Record money: expenses, credit paid back, cash in, cash out.
- Owed to the shop: each customer's credit balance, with a one-tap WhatsApp reminder that states the amount.
- Close the day in three steps (check, count, close), with a reason when the drawer is short or over, and an optional WhatsApp summary.
- Reopen a closed day; past days are read-only.
- A close-day reminder.

**Reports**
- Sales, profit, number of sales and average sale.
- A daily chart, how customers paid, and top products.
- Today, this week, this month, a custom range, or one day.
- Export to PDF and CSV.

**Safety**
- An optional PIN for voids, stock corrections, closing, reopening and restoring.
- Staff PINs: each person signs in with their own PIN, sales and voids record who made them, and Owner and Cashier roles.
- Backup to a single zip, and a checked restore.
- An automatic backup on the tablet after each closed day, keeping the last 7.

**Setup, layouts and languages**
- Onboarding that sets up the shop and opens the first day; product import from CSV.
- Tablet (landscape or portrait) and phone layouts, light and dark themes, up to 200% font size.
- English, Kiswahili and Portuguese. Luganda and Tigrinya are being translated.

## Arch-SME Cloud: paid, optional

| Plan | What you get | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Cloud Backup** | A backup in the cloud after each closed day. Restore onto a new tablet if yours is lost or broken. Backups kept for 90 days, plus one a month for a year. | First |
| **Owner** | Everything in Cloud Backup, plus the shop on any phone browser: today's sales and how customers paid, the cash count and whether it's short or over, voids and their reasons, owed to the shop, low stock, and staff activity. | Next |
| **Owner in the app** | The Owner plan inside the Arch-SME app, and managing staff PINs from your phone. | Later |
| **Business** | Several tills in one shop, and several branches. | Future |

- You sign in with your phone number and a code sent by SMS. Staff don't need an account.
- Plans are prepaid by Mobile Money, weekly or monthly. Nothing renews by itself. Prices will be announced at launch.
- New shops get a 30-day free trial.

**If you stop paying**
1. The cloud keeps working for 14 more days.
2. After that the cloud pauses. The app keeps working fully on the tablet.
3. Your cloud data is kept for 90 days, and you can download it at any time.
